## Ingredients

- 2 cups Fairlife Chocolate Milk
- 2 tbsp powdered peanut butter
- ½ scoop Isopure Chocolate Whey Isolate
- ¼ tsp xanthan gum

## Instructions

1. **Blend the Base:** Pour the Fairlife Chocolate Milk into your clean Ninja Creami pint. Add the powdered peanut butter, Isopure whey isolate, and xanthan gum. Use an immersion blender or handheld milk frother to thoroughly mix everything until completely smooth and free of powdery clumps.
2. **Freeze Flat:** Secure the storage lid tightly onto the pint. Place it on a completely level surface in your freezer and let it freeze undisturbed for a full 24 hours. Ensuring it freezes flat protects your machine's blade.
3. **The Initial Spin:** Remove the pint from the freezer and take off the lid. If a small frozen peak has formed in the center, scrape it flat with a spoon. Lock the pint into the outer bowl assembly, attach it to the Ninja Creami, and run it on the Lite Ice Cream cycle.
4. **The Splash & Re-Spin:** Once the cycle finishes, the texture will likely look dry, powdery, or crumbly. This is normal for high-protein bases. Dig a small well in the center of the pint, pour in 1 tablespoon of liquid Fairlife milk, lock it back in, and press the Re-Spin button.
5. **Serve:** Scoop out your ultra-thick, velvety chocolate peanut butter ice cream right away, or add your favorite allergen-safe mix-ins!

## Notes

~44g total protein per pint.

If you don't finish the pint in one sitting, smooth down the top surface with a spoon before putting it back in the freezer. When you want to eat it again, re-spin it on the machine to restore that perfect, creamy soft-serve texture.
