LifeOS Browser Extension

Syncs your Walmart order history & cart to LifeOS to speed up grocery cart building. Read-only — never places orders.

Get your token: while logged into LifeOS, open <LifeOS URL>/api/grocery/ingest-token in a tab and copy the token value.

Then: visit walmart.com/orders (and your cart) while signed in — syncing happens automatically. The toolbar badge shows OK/ERR.
