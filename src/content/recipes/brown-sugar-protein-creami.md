## Ingredients

- 2 cups Fairlife 2% Milk
- 3 tbsp Torani Sugar-Free Brown Sugar Syrup
- 1 scoop vanilla whey isolate (~25g protein)
- ¼ tsp xanthan gum

## Instructions

1. **Blend the Base:** Pour the Fairlife milk into your Ninja Creami pint. Add the vanilla whey isolate, Torani syrup, and xanthan gum. Use an immersion blender or milk frother to mix until completely smooth with no powdery clumps.
2. **Freeze Flat:** Secure the lid and place on a level surface in your freezer. Freeze undisturbed for a full 24 hours.
3. **The Initial Spin:** Remove from freezer, take off the lid, and flatten any center peak with a spoon. Lock the pint in and run on the Lite Ice Cream cycle.
4. **The Splash & Re-Spin:** If the result looks crumbly or dry (common with high-protein bases), dig a small well, add 1 tbsp Fairlife milk, and press Re-Spin.
5. **Serve:** Enjoy immediately, or smooth the top and return to the freezer — re-spin before eating again to restore texture.

## Notes

~51g total protein per pint (Fairlife 2% has ~13g per cup; adjust slightly if using whole milk).

Torani Brown Sugar Syrup gives a warm caramel-adjacent flavor. Start with 2 tbsp if you prefer less sweetness, or go up to 4 tbsp for a more pronounced taste.
